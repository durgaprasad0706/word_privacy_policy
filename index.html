Privacy Policy for Word Explorer

Application: Word Explorer • Package: com.aistudio.wordsearch.rkymzq • Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Word Explorer ("we", "our", or "us"). We value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our mobile application ("Word Explorer" or the "App"), how that information is used, and how it is protected in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver educational, relaxing word puzzle gameplay, track your game progression, support optional cloud backup, and manage in-app purchases.

A. Local Gameplay Data (Stored on Your Device)

The game saves your progress locally, including: current level, highest level unlocked, completed puzzles, daily streak dates, coin balance, hint count, unlocked wallpapers, and preferences (music, sound effects, and haptic feedback). This data is stored locally in your device's private app storage.

B. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync (Firebase)

You can choose to play offline or sign in with your Google Account using Google Play / Jetpack Credential Manager. If you choose to sign in, Firebase Authentication provides:

  • A unique user identification key (UID)
  • Your public display name and avatar URL
  • Your email address (for account identification only)

This allows us to sync your game progress to Firebase Cloud Firestore so you never lose your coins, levels, or streaks when switching devices.

C. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Word Explorer offers optional in-app purchases (such as coin packs, hint packs, and "Remove Ads"). All financial transactions and payment card details are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing. We never collect, see, or store your credit card or banking information. We only receive purchase tokens to verify purchase validity and unlock your items.

D. Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob)

Word Explorer integrates Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements (for free coins or hints). Google AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID / AAID)
  • Device type, operating system version, and general network state
  • Coarse location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction metrics to prevent ad fraud and reward completed video views

Purchasing the "Remove Ads" option removes banner and interstitial advertisements from your gameplay.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the game features (level progression, daily streak rewards, vocabulary flashcards).
  • To grant rewards when you watch rewarded video ads.
  • To fulfill in-app purchases and allow purchase restoration via Google Play.
  • To synchronize your game progress across your Android devices when signed in with Google.
  • To fix bugs, prevent cheating, and improve overall game performance.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates trusted, industry-standard third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing data handling:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play In-App Billing: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Word Explorer is an educational, family-friendly word puzzle game designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information directly from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information from our servers.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. All communications between the App and our backend servers are secured using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Cloud Firestore rules enforce zero-trust access control, ensuring that players can only access and update their own saved game records.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

- Local Data: You can completely erase all local game data at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android Settings > Apps > Word Explorer > Storage > Clear Storage.
- Cloud Data: If you signed in with Google and wish to delete your cloud profile and progress history from our Firestore database, you can send a deletion request to our support email at dp5824722@gmail.com with your Google Account email. Your data will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
